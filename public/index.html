How it works

From a ringing phone to a booked consultation

Four steps, all automatic, and every decision is saved so nothing depends on anyone's memory.

1

It answers

The voice agent picks up on the first ring, any hour, and listens in English, Hindi or Marathi.

2

It understands

It scores five things: a real project, service area, timeline, budget signals and who decides.

3

It decides

Qualify, ask one more question, decline kindly, or escalate a complaint. Hard rules can overrule the AI.

4

It hands over

A slot is booked, the designer gets a full note by email, and a deal appears in the CRM.

What it does

Built around how Aangan actually works

📞

Never misses a call

A third of enquiries arrive after 7pm. They all get answered, with replies in about three seconds.

🧠

Decides like the founder

Nikhil's own five-point rubric drives every decision, with the reasons stored for review.

🗣️

Speaks your customers' language

English, Hindi and Marathi, in the studio's warm, plain tone, one question at a time.

🚫

Never quotes a price

Pricing depends on the site, so every reply passes a filter that blocks any figure before it is spoken.

📅

Books the consultation

Reads what the caller said about their availability and books a real slot in the designer's calendar.

📨

Hands over everything

Designers get the full brief by email, so their first call is never spent re-asking the basics.
